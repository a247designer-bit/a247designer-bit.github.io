When you visit our Sites, certain information is automatically collected, including:

Information contained in cookies placed on your device by Third-Party Platforms may be accessed by us in accordance with the terms of your agreement and privacy settings with such Third-Party Platforms. Part III – Information Sharing Practices: How We Share Your Information

Disclosure of Information to Small and Medium Businesses (SMBs)

Upon making an appointment through our Sites, the information provided by you will be disclosed to us and the SMB with whom you have chosen to schedule the appointment. This disclosure is necessary for the purpose of facilitating your appointment and is akin to the disclosure that would occur if you made an appointment by calling the SMB, emailing the SMB, or utilizing the SMB's website. Should you provide a mobile phone number, the SMBs may send you text messages pertaining to your reservation. Some SMBs may also require your credit or debit card account details to secure your appointment. When appointments are made through our Sites, we may additionally share additional information with the SMBs, such as your historical information or information collected through Third-Party Platforms. Furthermore, you have the option to indicate special preferences or provide comments about your appointment, which Blookd will transmit to the respective SMB. We may also furnish SMBs with summary reports of client feedback. Should you choose to provide comments about an SMB through our Sites, these comments may be shared with the SMB. While we will not associate your comments with identifiable information, an SMB may discern your identity from the comments, particularly if you disclose your name or provide contact information, such as an email address. Information that you choose to share with an SMB when making an appointment and/or making a payment through our Sites may be utilized by the SMB for its own purposes. Blookd does not exercise control over the privacy practices of SMBs. Kindly reach out to the SMB directly if you wish to acquaint yourself with their privacy practices.
